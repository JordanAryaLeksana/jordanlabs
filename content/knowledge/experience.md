# Experience

## IT Intern (extended) — PT Pertamina EP Cepu (Jan 2025 – May 2025)

The official internship ran from 10 January to 14 February 2025 at the Jambaran Tiung Biru gas plant; Jordan continued the project until May 2025.

Location: Cepu, Central Java, Indonesia.

- Built an LSTM-Autoencoder anomaly detector in Python and TensorFlow on a year of 15-minute operating data from six gas-plant solvent pumps. In 2023 a mechanical-seal failure on one of these pumps had caused a USD 393K plant shutdown. The detector flagged 5,800+ anomalies, and the operations team verified them against actual plant events.
- Trained a decision-tree classifier (73% F1, versus 12% for logistic regression) that labels each anomaly as start-up, nomination change, process change or equipment fault, using categories agreed with field operations.
- Wrote a Python rule engine that checks pump operation against the manufacturer's head, NPSHr and power-versus-flow curves and is designed to raise email alerts and maintenance work orders.
- The classifier and rule engine are the next-phase features of LEADS (Leading Equipment Anomaly Detection System), the plant's system described in SPE-224966-MS, which estimates savings of up to USD 476K. Jordan contributed to the methodology and manuscript; he is not a listed author. The project is documented in the portfolio as PumpSentinel.
